VaultBridge Finance 78 – AML/KYC Policy (Summary)

1. OWNERSHIP
VaultBridge Finance 78 ownership is held through a nominee arrangement managed by a Seychelles fiduciary. UBO documentation is held by the fiduciary and has not been provided.

2. AML CONTROLS
Manual transaction review process. No automated monitoring system currently deployed.
Customer Due Diligence applied to all onboarding.
Enhanced Due Diligence for transactions > EUR 10,000.

3. PEP SCREENING
No politically exposed persons have been identified among key personnel or beneficial owners.

4. REGULATORY HISTORY
A minor AML procedural warning was issued by the Estonian FSA in 2022. Remediation completed.


5. DATA PRIVACY
GDPR compliance programme underway. DPIA not yet completed.
