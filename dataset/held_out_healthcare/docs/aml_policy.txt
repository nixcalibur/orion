Meridian Health Assurance FZE - AML/CFT Policy

1. POLICY STATUS
This formal AML/CFT policy is approved by the board and in force. The Money Laundering Reporting Officer has been appointed (MLRO: T. Rahman).
Customer due diligence applies to all onboarding. Enhanced due diligence applies to transactions above AED 100,000.

2. SCREENING
All customers and beneficial owners are screened at onboarding. No politically exposed persons have been identified among key personnel or beneficial owners.

3. DATA PROTECTION
GDPR-aligned programme. DPIA completed. Data Protection Officer appointed.
