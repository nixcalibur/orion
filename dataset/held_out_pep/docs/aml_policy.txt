Rivergate Payments Ltd – Compliance Manual (excerpt)

1. CONTROLS
Customer due diligence is applied at onboarding. Transaction monitoring covers
wire transfers above EUR 5,000. A formal AML/CFT policy is in force.

2. POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS
CEO Helena Varga previously served as a deputy minister of finance (2014–2018)
and is treated as a PEP under firm screening procedures. Screening runs against
World-Check. Supporting PEP diligence materials are stated to be kept on record
with the MLRO; no separate PEP diligence file is included in this submission pack.

3. REGULATORY HISTORY
In 2021 the Bank of Lithuania issued an administrative warning for a late
quarterly return. The firm paid the administrative fee and the matter was
formally closed with no further action.

4. DATA PROTECTION
GDPR-aligned processing. DPIA on file. DPO appointed.
