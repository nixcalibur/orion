OpenVault Technologies 49 – Beneficial Ownership Declaration

OpenVault Technologies 49 is 100% owned by OpenVault Technologies 49 Holdings BV (Netherlands).
OpenVault Technologies 49 Holdings BV is owned 55% by Alpine Trust SA (Luxembourg) and 45% by Henrik Larsson (natural person, SE).
Alpine Trust SA is a discretionary trust. Trustee: Helvetica Fiduciaries AG.
Beneficiaries of the trust have not been disclosed in this submission.
UBO documentation for the trust is held by the trustee and is available on request from the MFSA.
